nudg

The interaction format your AI can nudge. Each component below is played from a plain JSON file by the same headless core.

Toggle

The knob springs; the color tweens. Tap again mid-flight: the spring keeps its velocity.

toggle.nudg.json

Toggle with press

Two parallel regions: checked, and a knob that shrinks while pressed. Turning off settles without overshoot.

toggle-press.nudg.json

Like

A kick adds velocity to the heart's scale without changing its target, so it pops and settles. The motions come from an imported brand library.

like.nudg.json

Draggable toggle

Drag the knob, or flick it. On release it springs to where the throw was heading, carrying your finger's speed. The color is mapped from the knob's position.

toggle-drag.nudg.json

Swipe row

Swipe left to reveal Delete. A short or slow swipe snaps back; a long or quick one stays open. The action fades in through a map.
